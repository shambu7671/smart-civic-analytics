ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES

Key Technological Highlights

Built from foundational civic engineering principles to replace redundant complaints with verifiable, data-backed city issues.

Citizen Photo & Geotagging

Streamlined reporting interface with live browser GPS geolocation, interactive pin dragging, image MIME validation, and self-reported severity inputs.

Computer Vision Detection

YOLO-compatible architecture configured for potholes, road cracks, and surface damage with robust fallback handling and confidence thresholding.

Explainable Duplicate Detection

Combines PostGIS spatial radius queries, category matching, perceptual image hashing, and TF-IDF text similarity into transparent similarity scores.

Haversine DBSCAN Clustering

Groups multiple citizen complaints within a physical threshold (e.g. 80m) into a single operational civic issue to avoid overwhelming public works.

Normalized Dynamic Prioritization

Calculates priority scores (0–100) dynamically considering severity, report density, ticket age, and location importance with admin-configurable weights.

Interactive GIS & Heatmaps

Interactive Leaflet maps for both administrators and the public. Exposes verified issues and density heatmaps while strictly keeping citizen identities private.
