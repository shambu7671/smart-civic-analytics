SYSTEM SPECIFICATION

Architecture & Technical Methodology

Comprehensive architectural overview of the AI-Based Smart Civic Issue Detection & Complaint Analytics system.

Research Motivation & Scope

Conventional municipal complaint portals suffer from ticket fragmentation, duplicate submissions, and subjective priority assignment. This academic prototype bridges citizen input and automated municipal operations by deploying computer vision, spatial clustering, and multi-factor mathematical prioritization.

Explainability

Similarity vectors (distance, category, image hash, TF-IDF text) are stored distinctly, allowing administrators to audit why reports are flagged as duplicates.

Meter-Based GIS

Uses PostgreSQL GEOGRAPHY(POINT, 4326) and PostGIS spatial indexing for real-world haversine distance calculations without planar distortion.

Mathematical Formulations

1. Priority Score Normalization

Calculated as a normalized linear combination of four configurable parameters (weights dynamically normalized to sum to 1.0):

Score = (w_sev × Severity) + (w_rep × Reports) + (w_age × Age) + (w_loc × Location)

2. DBSCAN Spatial Clustering

Groups nearby reports into unified issues using the haversine metric:

d(lat1, lon1, lat2, lon2) ≤ ε (Configurable threshold: 80 meters)
